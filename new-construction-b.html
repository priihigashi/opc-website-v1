The OPC way

One crew. No handoffs. No surprises.

Most "custom home" builds are really a general contractor managing a rotating cast of subs — and every handoff is a place for the budget, the schedule, and the quality to slip.

We do it differently. Oak Park Construction self-performs the core trades and keeps an owner on site every day, so the person who shipped your foundation is accountable for the home you move into. Licensed Florida contractor, CBC1263425, serving Broward, Palm Beach & Miami-Dade.

Questions

What people ask before they build.

How long does a custom home take?

Most custom homes run 10–16 months from permit to keys depending on size, site, and selections. We give you a real schedule up front and update it as we go.

Do you handle design and permits, or just build?

Both. We're design-build — architectural plans, engineering, permitting, and construction all under one roof, so nothing falls through the cracks between firms.

What areas do you build in?

Broward (our base), Palm Beach, and Miami-Dade — including Fort Lauderdale, Boca Raton, Coral Springs, Weston, Miami, and surrounding cities.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — licensed Florida general contractor CBC1263425, fully insured. Both owners are on the job, not a call center.
