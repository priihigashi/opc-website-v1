Questions

What people ask first.

How long does a full renovation take?

Whole-home renovations typically run 4–9 months depending on scope. We give you a real schedule up front.

Can you change the layout / move walls?

Yes — we handle structural changes, engineering, and permits so you get the floor plan you actually want.

Do we need to move out?

For a full gut, usually yes; for a partial, often no. We'll tell you honestly during the consult.

What areas do you serve?

Broward, Palm Beach, and Miami-Dade — Fort Lauderdale, Boca Raton, Coral Springs, Weston, Miami, and surrounding cities.
