Questions

What people ask first.

How long does a bathroom remodel take?

Most bathrooms run 3–5 weeks depending on scope and whether the layout changes. We give you a real schedule up front.

Why does waterproofing matter so much?

It's what keeps water out of your walls and floor. Done wrong, you get mold and rot behind beautiful tile. We build the system, not just the surface.

Can you do a tub-to-shower conversion?

Yes — it's one of our most common jobs. Curbless options, benches, niches, and frameless glass all on the table.

What areas do you serve?

Broward, Palm Beach, and Miami-Dade — Fort Lauderdale, Boca Raton, Coral Springs, Weston, Miami, and surrounding cities.
