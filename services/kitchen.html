Questions

What people ask first.

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

Most kitchens run 4–8 weeks depending on scope and whether walls move. We give you a real schedule up front.

Do you actually build the cabinets?

Yes — we build and install custom cabinetry, not flat-pack boxes. Real carcasses, soft-close, sized to your room.

Can we stay in the house during the remodel?

Usually yes. We dust-control and protect the rest of the home, though the kitchen itself is out of service during the build.

What areas do you serve?

Broward, Palm Beach, and Miami-Dade — Fort Lauderdale, Boca Raton, Coral Springs, Weston, Miami, and surrounding cities.
