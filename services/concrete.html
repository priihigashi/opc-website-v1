Questions

What people ask first.

Do you pour your own concrete?

Yes — our own crew forms, places steel, pours, and finishes. No mystery subs on the most important part of the build.

How long before I can use a new driveway?

Concrete is walkable in 24–48 hours and ready for vehicles in about a week as it cures.

Do concrete jobs need permits?

Structural slabs and many driveways do — we handle permits and inspections.

What areas do you serve?

Broward, Palm Beach, and Miami-Dade — Fort Lauderdale, Boca Raton, Coral Springs, Weston, Miami, and surrounding cities.
