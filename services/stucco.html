WHY STUCCO FAILS IN FLORIDA

Three problems we fix every week.

01

Hairline cracks

Florida heat + hurricane wind expand and contract stucco. Cracks let moisture in. We patch, mesh, and recoat to seal.

02

Water damage & delamination

Improper drainage planes cause stucco to bubble and fall off. We strip, install proper weather barrier, restucco.

03

Full recoat / restucco

Old textures look dated. New color coats with elastomeric finishes look fresh + last 15+ years in Florida sun.
