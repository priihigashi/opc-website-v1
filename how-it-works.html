The process

How a project actually runs with us.

01

Consult

We walk the site, talk through what you actually want, and give an honest budget — before anything's promised. If we're not the right fit, we'll tell you.

02

Design & Estimate

Plans, selections, and a line-item estimate. No vague "allowances" that quietly balloon halfway through. You see where every dollar goes.

03

Permit

We handle the engineering, the paperwork, and the city — Broward, Palm Beach, or Miami-Dade. You stay informed, not buried in bureaucracy.

04

Build

One crew, owner on site daily, core trades self-performed. You get progress photos and a real answer when you call — not a runaround between subs.

05

Walk-Through & Keys

We finish the punch list, hand over the warranty, and you move into a home built to the standard we'd accept on our own.
