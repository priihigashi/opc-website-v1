## Recovery provenance and limitations

- IMG4626 original provenance is `IMG_4626.jpeg` in the historical catalog; public launch data intentionally omits the source filename and Drive identifiers.
- Addition cover IMG4755 and Salon cover IMG0998 were selected after direct visual comparison against their existing public alternatives.
- Airport recovery: IMG7277, IMG7239, IMG7191, IMG7140. Patio Slab recovery: IMG4783, IMG4794, IMG4698. All were visual-reviewed before restoration and have no readable residential identifier.
- The connected Photos app was opened for both libraries: Shared contained 1,059 photos and 755 videos; Personal contained 30,463 photos and 14,732 videos. Native search automation returned `AXError.cannotComplete`, and direct Photos-library metadata access was denied by macOS privacy controls (`Operation not permitted`). This does not show that the requested Pompano March 2025 finals are absent; it prevents reliable programmatic recovery from those libraries in this session.
- The Drive catalog recovered in this session contained 178 rows. Range sentinels were checked; that catalog is not treated as exhaustive of the owner’s personal or shared Photos libraries.
