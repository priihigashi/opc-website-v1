## Confidence contract

A number cannot override a hard gate. A group is publishable only when identity, phase, work type, rights/privacy, non-mixing, cover truth, and derivative existence are confirmed. Any conflict caps the group at 4/10 and holds it.

Soft score after hard gates: provenance 0–3; visual quality/naturalness 0–2; useful story 0–2; cover 0–1; route/copy/SEO 0–1; derivatives 0–1.

- 8–10: include in the review candidate.
- 6–7.9: hold for confirmation or improvement.
- 0–5.9 or hard-gate failure: exclude from today's candidate.
