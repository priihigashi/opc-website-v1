Who Builds It

Built around how you'd want your own home built.

Construction is a relationship business. The difference between a great project and a stressful one usually isn't the materials or the design — it's whether someone is actually paying attention every day, returning your calls, and treating your investment like it matters.

That's what Oak Park Construction was built around. We're a licensed Florida contractor (CBC1263425) serving Broward, Palm Beach, and Miami-Dade. We handle custom new builds, full home renovations, kitchens, bathrooms, additions, stucco, concrete, and outdoor living spaces.

We're hands-on. The owner is on every project. You get direct communication, daily progress photos, and a real answer when you call.
